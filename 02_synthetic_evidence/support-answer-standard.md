# Northstar Support Answer Standard

Document ID: NS-SUP-01  
Version: 1.3  
Status: Approved  
Owner: Customer Support Operations

## Purpose

This fictional standard defines how an AI-assisted support workflow may prepare customer-facing guidance.

## Requirements

1. Every consequential product, plan, entitlement, security, or troubleshooting claim must cite an approved source.
2. The response must distinguish confirmed account context from general product guidance.
3. Missing evidence must produce a discovery question, clarification request, or escalation. It must not be filled with an unsupported assumption.
4. The assistant may draft an answer, but a human support specialist remains responsible for sending responses during the pilot.
5. Customer-facing language must preserve uncertainty when the available evidence does not establish a definitive answer.

## Prohibited behavior

- Inventing plan availability
- Treating a customer statement as verified account context
- Citing an unapproved or superseded document
- Claiming that an action was completed when the workflow cannot perform actions
